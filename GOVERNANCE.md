# Governance

Runlease is maintained by the team behind [Marshal](https://marshal.codes). It is Apache-2.0
licensed and is developed in the open.

## Roles

- **Users** run Runlease and report what they find.
- **Contributors** send issues, documentation and pull requests.
- **Maintainers** review and merge changes, cut releases and handle security reports. They are
  listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

A contributor who has sent several good changes and reviews carefully may be invited to become a
maintainer by the existing maintainers.

## Decisions

Most decisions are made in the open, on issues and pull requests, by whoever is doing the work
with a maintainer's review. Where people disagree, the maintainers decide and say why in the
thread.

Changes to the security properties described in [docs/security.md](docs/security.md) need
agreement from every maintainer.

## Roadmap

The roadmap is the set of pinned issues on the repository. Anyone can propose an item by opening
an issue.

## Commercial relationship

Marshal sells a separate product. Runlease is not a trial of it and has no features held back for
paying customers.
