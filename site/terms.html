What the service is

A relay that carries connections between the Runlease command-line tool and machines you launched with it. It is offered free of charge, with limits, and without any promise that it will be available or keep running.

Limits

The free tier allows a small number of machines connected at once and a maximum connection time. The current numbers are shown by runlease whoami. We may change them.

Acceptable use

You may use the relay to reach machines you launched with Runlease, in cloud accounts you are entitled to use. You may not:

  • use it as a general-purpose proxy or to hide where traffic comes from;
  • use it for anything unlawful, or to attack, scan or disrupt systems you do not own;
  • try to get around the limits, including by using several accounts;
  • try to disrupt the relay or reach other people's connections.

We may suspend or remove access for an account that breaks these rules or puts the service at risk.
